# Shutdown Notice

Hi everyone,
This decision was not made lightly. Following a directive issued by the school board, we have been instructed to discontinue operations. In addition, we have faced ongoing challenges that have made it increasingly difficult to continue development and maintain the level of quality and support that our community deserves. After careful consideration, we have concluded that shutting down our services is the most appropriate course of action.

We would like to express our deepest gratitude to all players, supporters, and collaborators who have been part of this journey. Your enthusiasm, creativity, and loyalty have meant a great deal to us, and we are incredibly proud of the community that has grown around our projects.

All services, including game servers, updates, and customer support, will be discontinued as part of this closure. We encourage users to make any necessary arrangements prior to the shutdown.

Thank you once again for your support and understanding.
Sincerly,
